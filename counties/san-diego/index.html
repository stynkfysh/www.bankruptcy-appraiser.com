Bankruptcy Appraisals Across San Diego County

U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Southern District of California (San Diego)

San Diego County stretches from the beaches of Oceanside and Coronado to the mountain towns around Julian and Cuyamaca, and its housing is just as varied. A bankruptcy appraisal here might involve a condo in a large HOA complex near the coast, a 1920s bungalow in an older city neighborhood, a planned-community home with Mello-Roos assessments in the North County inland area, or a ranch property with a private well near Ramona. Each type has its own buyer pool and its own valuation pitfalls. Brian Ward has appraised throughout the county since 2004 and prepares every report himself. For Chapter 7 cases, he provides petition-date values that a trustee can test against the homestead exemption. For Chapter 13, he supports liquidation analysis, motions to value junior liens, and cramdown on rental property. For a home or condo, the Basic Desktop runs from $299 and the inspected Standard from $699, and you get a written quote before work starts. Call (760) 557-2147 to discuss a property anywhere in the county.

The court that hears these cases

Bankruptcy cases filed by San Diego County residents are heard in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of California, located in downtown San Diego. That district also covers Imperial County. Trustees assigned to San Diego cases review schedules with local market knowledge, so a value that looks out of line with nearby sales tends to draw questions. Meetings of creditors are often conducted by video. Your attorney will handle filings and court procedures; Brian's role is to supply a well-supported opinion of value as of the date counsel specifies, and to explain or testify about it if needed.
