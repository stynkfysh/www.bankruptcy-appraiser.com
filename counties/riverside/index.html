Bankruptcy Appraisals Across Riverside County

U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Central District of California, Riverside Division

Riverside County covers a large and varied territory, and a bankruptcy valuation here can look very different from one town to the next. Southwest county communities such as Temecula, Murrieta, and Menifee are dominated by newer planned subdivisions, many with HOA dues and special assessments. The city of Riverside, Corona, and the Jurupa Valley mix older neighborhoods with infill and tract housing. The San Jacinto Valley includes established retirement communities and manufactured homes, while Anza and Aguanga bring rural parcels with wells and septic systems. Out in the Coachella Valley, golf-course homes, country-club condos, and a seasonal buyer market raise their own timing questions for a petition-date value. Since 2004 Brian Ward has valued property throughout the county, and he writes each report himself. He provides Chapter 7 schedule values, Chapter 13 liquidation support, and evidence for motions to value. Fees start at $299, are fixed in a written quote, and are due on delivery. Call (760) 557-2147.

The court that hears these cases

Riverside County residents file in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Central District of California, Riverside Division, which sits in the city of Riverside. The Central District is divided into several divisions, and the Riverside Division is the one that hears cases filed by Riverside County residents. Your attorney manages filings, local rules, and hearings. Brian supplies a value as of the date counsel requests, written so a trustee or judge can follow the reasoning, and he is available to testify if a valuation dispute reaches a hearing.
