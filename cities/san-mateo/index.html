Homes in San Mateo

San Mateo sits midway down the Peninsula, with older neighborhoods near downtown and newer areas toward the bay and the hills. Baywood, Aragon, and San Mateo Park have large, mature lots and homes from the 1920s through the 1940s, including Tudor, Spanish, and traditional styles. North Central and the areas around downtown hold smaller homes, duplexes, and apartment buildings. East of Highway 101, Shoreview and the bayfront lagoon communities offer mid-century homes and townhouses, some near water. Laurelwood and Sugarloaf climb into the western hills with split-levels and view homes. Condominiums are common near Caltrain stations and along El Camino Real. Lot size, era, and proximity to the creek or the bay can all influence a San Mateo petition-date value.

How a desktop petition-date value works here

A desktop for a San Mateo property begins with what the record already shows. Brian reviews the assessor data and the home's listing history, often with photos from earlier sales that reveal how much has changed since. City permits confirm additions, remodels, and foundation work, which are common in the older neighborhoods. Aerial and street views show lot size, tree cover, slope in the western hills, and whether a bayside property backs onto water. Flood zone status is checked for the low-lying areas east of the freeway. Because there is no inspection, the owner or attorney provides current photos and a candid description of condition, including any repairs that are needed. Brian's license as a California certified residential appraiser covers San Mateo County. The report says it is a desktop with no inspection and gives its effective date, usually the petition date. Only desktop reports are sold in San Mateo; Standard and Drive-By are not.

San Mateo desktop appraisal questions

Is a desktop appropriate for a high-value San Mateo home?

It can be, though larger or unusual homes often fall outside the starting fee because comparables are harder to find. Brian will give you a written quote before beginning. If the case is likely to be contested and the other side insists on an inspection, a local appraiser may be the better choice.

Can my attorney's paralegal handle the photos and documents?

Yes. Many law offices coordinate the details: they gather photos from the client, send the HOA or lease documents, and confirm the effective date. Brian works directly with the firm, and firms can be invoiced. The report is delivered as a PDF ready for the file.
