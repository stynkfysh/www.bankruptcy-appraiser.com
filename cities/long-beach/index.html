Homes in Long Beach

Long Beach is a port city with a remarkable spread of housing. Along the shore, Belmont Shore, Naples, and Belmont Heights hold beach cottages, canal-front homes, and condo buildings, while downtown and the East Village add high-rise and loft condos. Bixby Knolls and California Heights in the north feature 1920s and 1930s Spanish Revival and Tudor homes on tree-lined streets. East Long Beach neighborhoods such as Los Altos and the Plaza were built after World War II with ranch homes on regular lots. Central and west Long Beach have a large share of duplexes, fourplexes, and courtyard buildings. Older brick buildings may carry seismic retrofit history, coastal homes may face flood zone questions, and the city has historic districts where exterior changes are reviewed. Proximity to the port, refineries, and freeways also affects buyer appeal.

How a desktop petition-date value works here

In Long Beach, Brian works by desktop only, so Standard and Drive-By reports are not available. For a petition-date value, he chooses closed sales near the filing date from the same neighborhood, because a canal home in Naples and a ranch home in Los Altos have little in common. He reviews prior listings for the subject, assessor records, and permits for additions or unit counts. Aerial and street views show water frontage, alley garages, parking, and exterior condition. For condos, HOA dues, special assessments, and building repairs you share help him compare units fairly. For a small rental, leases and rent figures let him consider income. Owner or attorney photos supply the interior picture he would otherwise get on site. Brian's California certified license is valid across the state. The report says no inspection was made and lists its effective date. Cases needing an inspected report should use a local appraiser.

Long Beach desktop appraisal questions

Can a Long Beach fourplex be appraised by desktop for Chapter 13?

Yes. Buildings holding two to four units are what the Desktop 2-4 Unit report was made for, with pricing from $549. Share rent amounts, lease terms, and photos of each unit. Your attorney can decide whether a desktop scope is suitable for any valuation motion or plan analysis in your case.

My condo building had a special assessment. Should I mention it?

Yes. A special assessment can affect what a buyer would pay for a unit, and Brian cannot learn about it from sales data alone. Send the HOA notice or a summary, along with your current dues, and he will consider it in the petition-date value.
