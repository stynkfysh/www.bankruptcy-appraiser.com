Twelve report types

Appraisal reports for every part of a case

Bankruptcy first, plus the divorce, estate, tax and dispute work that often travels with it.

Bankruptcy Appraisal

A defensible value for your home as of the petition date, written for Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 cases.

Date-of-Death Appraisal

A retrospective market value fixed to the day an owner passed, documented for tax and court use.

Divorce Appraisal

A neutral home value both spouses and their attorneys can rely on when dividing community property.

Estate & Trust Appraisal

Clear values for trustees and executors who must inventory, divide, sell or distribute real estate.

Expert Witness Appraisal

Litigation-ready appraisals and clear testimony from a certified appraiser with two decades of local work.

Family Transfer Appraisal

A documented value when a home passes between parents, children, siblings or other relatives.

Immigration I-864 Appraisal

Documented home equity for sponsors who use real estate as an asset on the Affidavit of Support.

Insurance Claim Appraisal

Market value evidence before and after damage, for homeowners and attorneys working through a property claim.

Pre-Listing Appraisal

An independent value before you list, so the price starts from evidence rather than optimism.

Pre-Purchase Appraisal

An independent check on price before you commit, especially when no lender will order an appraisal.

Property Tax Appeal Appraisal

Evidence for a decline-in-value review or assessment appeal, built around the January 1 lien date.

Bail Bond Property Appraisal

A quick, well-supported value for a family home being put up as collateral for a bail bond or property bond.
