Fee schedule

What a bankruptcy appraisal costs

Starting fees, written quotes, and the factors that move a price.

Bankruptcy appraisal fees are set in writing before any work starts, so you and your attorney know the cost up front. The figures below are starting fees for typical properties. Larger or more complex homes, luxury properties, acreage, and older retrospective effective dates are quoted above these floors, and the quote explains why. You pay once the report is delivered, using a card, a check or a bank transfer. Law firms ordering on behalf of clients can be invoiced. Testimony and depositions are billed separately. Rush turnaround is often available when a filing date or hearing is close.

Floors, not averages. The written quote is the price.

Desktop reports: $299 and $449

Desktop reports are completed without a site visit, using public records, listing history, photographs, and closed sales. The Basic Desktop at $299 offers a supported value for straightforward situations, while the Desktop at $449 adds more depth to the analysis. They work well for routine Chapter 7 schedules where equity is clearly below the exemption and a dispute is unlikely. A Desktop 2 to 4 unit report is $549.

Drive-By: $649

A Drive-By includes an exterior viewing of the property and its surroundings from the street. It confirms the condition of the roof, siding, yard, and setting, and verifies that the home matches public records. It is useful when interior access is not available, such as some trustee assignments, or when the main questions are about the exterior and location rather than interior finishes.

Inspected Standard: $699 and $799

With the Standard report you get a complete inspection inside and out, along with measurements and photos. Costing $699 on a house or condo, or $799 when the building has 2 to 4 units, this report is the most persuasive report for close equity questions, homes in poor condition, disputes over liquidation analysis and motions to value a second mortgage. If a case may be contested, this is usually the report to choose.

Testimony and extra dates

If a valuation is challenged and your attorney needs Brian at a deposition or evidentiary hearing, that time, including preparation, is billed separately from the report fee and discussed in advance. When a case needs values at more than one date, such as petition and confirmation, mention it when you order; the work can often be planned together and quoted as one engagement.

Tell Brian the address and the petition date. You will have a written quote, usually the same day.
