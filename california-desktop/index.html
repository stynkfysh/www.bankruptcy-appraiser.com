Desktop Bankruptcy Appraisals for Filers Elsewhere in California

Outside our San Diego and Riverside County service communities, every report is a desktop appraisal, with no site visit.

What is available outside San Diego and Riverside Counties

Basic Desktop (from $299), Desktop (from $449) and Desktop 2–4 Unit (from $549). Inspected Standard and Drive-By reports are not offered in these cities.

Inspected reports (Standard and Drive-By) are offered only in the San Diego County and Riverside County communities listed on this site. Everywhere else in California, Brian prepares desktop reports only; his certification is statewide, and a desktop appraisal does not require a site visit. That means filers and attorneys in other parts of the state may be able to obtain a supported petition-date value for Schedule A/B or a Chapter 13 liquidation analysis without waiting for a local appointment. Pricing starts at $299 for the Basic Desktop, $449 for the Desktop, and $549 for the 2 to 4 unit Desktop report. Before accepting an assignment outside his usual area, Brian confirms that he has reliable access to sales data and enough familiarity with the local market to do the work competently, as professional standards require. If he does not, he will say so.

Desktop reports are best suited to typical homes where the equity picture is reasonably clear and a contested hearing is not expected. If your case involves significant condition problems, a lien-stripping motion, or a likely valuation dispute, an inspected report from an appraiser near the property is usually the stronger choice, and Brian will tell you that directly. To ask about a property elsewhere in California, call (760) 557-2147 or email contact@bankruptcy-appraiser.com with the address, the effective date your attorney needs, and the court district if known. You will receive a written quote before any work starts. This information is general and not legal advice; your bankruptcy attorney decides how a value is used in your case.
